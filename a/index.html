What is Local Vending?

Local Vending KC is a vendor, retailer, wholesaler and co-packer. We make and distribute a family of more than five food and drink brands, then put them where Kansas City already is. Every market gets its own city letters, Local Vending (KC), so it is always truly local.

🥗

Vend

Fresh ready‑made food and drinks in machines at coffee shops, luxury apartments and other prime spots.

🧇

Make

Sauces, milks and waffles produced for wholesale and retail, and co‑packed for our brands.

🥛

Serve

The Moloko Milk Bar: coolers of sandwiches, sides, fresh juices, fruit milks and herbal‑infused milks.
