What is Local Vending?

Local Vending KC is a vendor, retailer, wholesaler and co-packer. We make and distribute a family of more than five food and drink brands, and we place them in vending machines across the city. Each location carries its own city letters after the name, Local Vending (KC), so every market is truly local.

Vend

Fresh ready-made food and drinks in machines placed in coffee shops, luxury apartments and other high-traffic spots.

Make

Sauces, milks and waffles produced for wholesale and retail, and co-packed for our brands.

Serve

The Moloko Milk Bar: coolers of sandwiches, sides, fresh juices, fruit milks and herbal-infused milks, plus soft serve.

Where we're headed

  1. Start: new vending accounts in Kansas City coffee shops.
  2. Next: a walk-up space for ready-made sandwiches, drinks and frozen pints. No seating.
  3. Then: manufacturing sauces, milks and waffles for wholesale and retail, sold through our machines too.

Opening-day favorite: an Ice Cream Sunday every week, with fresh-baked organic bread, pizza, sandwiches, soft serve and toppings.
